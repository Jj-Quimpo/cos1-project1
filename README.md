### Week 3

**Overview**: This week I continued work on the combat system and made some significant changes to the player class making the player start with a splintered spear to represent the ATK and a soda tab buckler to represent the DEF, and with the inclusion of the rat type class this holds the normal enemies you would face during an ambush as well as the different boss types the player can encounter, finally made a merchant class so the player can spend their crumbs buying healing items and upgrading the spear or shield or both if they can afford it.

**Improvements**: There is variety during combat featuring the Lowly Rat for floors before 5 an elite rat for floors before 10 and Rat Knights after 10 floors. Instead of completely getting rid of the lower rats I am instead adding different types of rats to the spawn pool (spawnRoll in my code) the boss appears every 10 floors but the floor before is a guaranteed merchant floor to give the player a chance to make any last minute upgrades or purchases. Also updated the inventory system so players can store and  use their healing items.

**Challenges**: I made a vector to display different floor descriptions but they were not showing, it took a while to realize I forgot to include a whole line to even display it, Now that I am including comments I can find where I want to work more easily but still get lost.

**Feedback**: The balancing is very important for the enjoyment of my game, so i've made the difficulty scale with how many floors the players survived and gradually adding either different types of enemies, increasing crumb rewards and guaranteeing certain floors.

**Next Steps**: Im going to make it so the player can view their inventory inbetween floors, use healing items inbetween floors, and an option for player to leave/go back to main meny inbetween floors, I am also wanting to make a leaderboard system so players can see what is their record.
